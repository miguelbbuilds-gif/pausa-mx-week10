# Demo script

About two and a half minutes. Speak in English. The screen is in Spanish. Do not describe the example as a real customer, an interview, or a pilot.

## Opening — landing page

[Open the local site. Stay on the start page.]

This is PAUSA MX, a buyer-side prototype. It does not sell anything and it does not connect to a bank. The promise on the page is that a suggestion is explained, can be dismissed, and does not hide a happiness score. The receipt on the right is already labeled fictional.

## Load the example — Demo

[Open Demo. Read the fictional banner. Click “Cargar este ejemplo” under “No compres por ahora.”]

I am loading the clothing example. Income is 12,000 pesos. Essentials are 7,000, discretionary spending is 2,000, and debt already in the month is 2,000. The jacket costs 3,000, in three payments of 1,000. There is also a fictional headphones installment of 500. The inclusion rule says that 500 already sits inside the 2,000, so it is not added a second time.

## The suggestion — Evaluate

[Click “Ver la sugerencia de este ejemplo.”]

The monthly surplus is exactly zero: 12,000 minus 7,000, minus 2,000, minus 2,000, minus the new 1,000. I am not going to call that negative 1,000. The negative 1,000 is a different line. If the person paid the whole 3,000 today from 2,000 in cash, the cash balance would be short by 1,000. Cash is not treated as income, and it is not added on top of the installment.

Because the surplus is not above zero, the suggestion is “NO COMPRES POR AHORA.” The page says this is not certainty. The assumptions list what was typed and what was left out, including any debt the person never entered.

## The person decides — same screen

[Select “Quiero registrarla como: podría comprar.” Leave “Conservar este registro” checked. Click “Guardar mi decisión.”]

I am overriding the suggestion on purpose. The app records that I chose “you could buy,” and it still shows that the engine said not to buy. It does not block the choice, and it does not rewrite the arithmetic. On the decisions page both lines remain visible.

[Point at the retention sentence.]

I kept this record, so a follow-up can appear after 30 real days. I am not going to pretend those days have passed. If I had not kept it, the record would expire and the app would not invent an answer.

## Close — Payments, briefly

[Open Payments.]

The calendar shows the headphones and the proposed jacket payments side by side. The sentence under the inclusion rule says the amount added this month is zero, while the profile total of 2,000 is what the surplus used. That is the guardrail: a manual schedule, without counting the same debt twice, and without reading any other installment app.

[Stop. Do not show a fake interview result.]
