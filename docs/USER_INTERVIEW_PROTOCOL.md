# User interview protocol

This is a plan for a future consented conversation. It is not a transcript. No interview was conducted for PAUSA MX, and this document contains no findings, quotes, or satisfaction scores.

## Who may be invited

An adult who has considered a purchase they might pay over time, and who can look at a prototype in Spanish. Do not recruit through a promise of money from a lender, a merchant, or this project. Do not interview anyone whose participation would depend on buying something.

## Consent script

Read this before opening the prototype. Stop if the person does not agree.

> Quiero mostrarte un prototipo llamado PAUSA MX. No es un banco, no es una tienda y no es asesoría financiera. Los números que escribas se quedan en este navegador. No voy a guardarlos en un servidor y no voy a publicarlos con tu nombre.
>
> Te voy a pedir que mires una compra de ejemplo y, si quieres, que escribas cifras tuyas. Puedes usar cifras inventadas. No tienes que decir tu ingreso real.
>
> Puedes dejar de participar en cualquier momento, saltarte una pregunta y pedirme que borre los datos del navegador antes de irte. No hay respuesta correcta. No estoy midiendo si compras o no.
>
> ¿Quieres continuar?

If they say no, stop. Do not persuade them. If they say yes, note only the date and that consent was given. Do not record their name in the prototype.

## Before the tasks

- Ask whether they want to use fictional numbers.
- Show the fictional banner if the demo is loaded.
- Remind them the suggestion can be ignored.
- Do not tell them which outcome you hope to see.

## Tasks

1. From the start page, find where to enter monthly numbers. Say what you think this tool is for.
2. Open the fictional “do not buy for now” example. Say, in your own words, what the zero surplus means and what the separate cash figure means.
3. Find the place where you could record a different decision from the suggestion. You do not have to change it.
4. Open Payments and say whether the listed example payment is being added on top of the monthly debt total.
5. Find how to delete the data in this browser.

## Questions

Ask one at a time. Do not fill silence with a preferred answer.

- What were you trying to decide while you looked at this?
- What number did you trust, and what number did you not trust?
- Was anything presented as more certain than the information you typed?
- Did you feel pushed toward buying, waiting, or refusing? What made you feel that?
- What would you need to see before you used this with your own money?
- What should this tool refuse to do?

If the person asks “so should I buy it?”, answer that the prototype is not advice and return to what they see on the screen.

## What not to ask

- Don’t ask “You see that you should not buy, right?”
- Don’t ask them to rank merchants.
- Don’t ask for bank passwords, card numbers, or a live login.
- Don’t ask them to complete a purchase.
- Don’t ask a minor to discuss their finances or anyone else’s.

## Afterward

- Offer to delete the browser data while they watch.
- Write down what they did and the words they used. Separate your interpretation from their words.
- Do not add a follow-up answer in the app. If 30 days have not passed, the app will not show that question. Do not pretend that it did.
- A later conversation, if they still consent, can ask whether the decision still feels right. That later conversation is not done until it happens.

## Records

Store notes outside the prototype, without their name, unless they explicitly ask to be identified. This repository does not contain interview notes because none exist.
